# 🎙️ TalkPaste - Speak into any desktop application easily

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-TalkPaste-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Buttegenushydrophyllum403/TalkPaste)

TalkPaste turns spoken words into text inside any application on your computer. It uses your computer hardware to process speech. This means your voice data stays on your machine. You do not send audio to a cloud server. The software listens when you press your chosen key, transcribes the audio, and pastes the result directly where your cursor sits.

## 🛠️ System Requirements

To run TalkPaste, your computer needs the following specifications:

*   **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   **Processor:** A modern processor with at least 4 cores.
*   **Memory:** 8 GB of RAM or more.
*   **Storage:** 2 GB of free space for the application and transcription models.
*   **Microphone:** Any standard computer microphone or headset connected via USB or jack.

## ⚙️ Initial Configuration

After installation, launch TalkPaste from your Start menu. The application will open a small window or run in your system tray at the bottom right of your taskbar.

1. **Select Microphone:** Open the settings menu inside the application. Ensure your preferred microphone appears in the input device list.
2. **Choose a Hotkey:** Set a push-to-talk key. This is the button you hold while speaking. Most users choose the Left Control key or a side button on their mouse.
3. **Model Selection:** The application includes a base transcription model. You can download larger models within the settings menu if you need higher accuracy. These larger models require more memory.
4. **Test:** Open a text editor like Notepad. Hold your chosen hotkey, speak a clear sentence, and release the key. The text will appear in your editor after a short delay.

## 💡 How It Works

TalkPaste uses a technology called Whisper. This system converts sound waves into written characters. Because this process runs offline, the software requires a small amount of your computer's power. It does not record your conversations when you do not hold the hotkey. You maintain full control over your data because no internet connection is necessary for the transcription process.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Security

Privacy remains the core focus of this project. Many dictation tools upload your audio to a server. These servers store your voice to train their systems. TalkPaste changes this process. It performs every calculation on your internal hardware. Data never leaves your computer. If you disconnect your internet cable, the software continues to work without issues.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the software does not work as expected, check these common fixes:

*   **No Text Appears:** Ensure you are holding the hotkey firmly before you begin speaking. Wait for a small indicator or sound cue if the application provides one.
*   **Slow Transcription:** Transcription requires a computer with a decent processor. Close heavy programs like video games or image editors if you notice a delay in text input.
*   **Low Accuracy:** Check your microphone position. Background noise confuses the transcription engine. Speak at a normal volume and pace. If you use a very loud environment, consider a noise-canceling microphone.
*   **Installation Error:** If Windows prevents the file from running, click "More Info" on the blue prompt window and then select "Run anyway." This is common for new applications.

## 📈 Performance Tips

*   Use a wired connection for your microphone if possible. Bluetooth microphones occasionally introduce latency, which leads to chopped audio.
*   Test different transcription models. The "Small" model offers a balance between speed and accuracy for most users. The "Large" model is very accurate but requires a more powerful computer.
*   Keep the application window open in the background. It consumes very little power when it is not actively transcribing.

## 📝 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does TalkPaste require an internet connection?**
No. You only need the internet to download the initial installer. After that, you never need the web to transcribe speech.

**Can I use this for multiple languages?**
Yes. The software supports many languages. You can change your target language in the settings menu.

**Will this interfere with my other keys?**
The software only listens for your assigned push-to-talk key while it is active. It does not block other programs from using that key for their own functions while TalkPaste is idle.

**Is my data stored?**
TalkPaste creates a temporary file in your computer memory during the transcription process. It deletes this file immediately after it pastes the text into your chosen application.
